    Help & Guidance

    How to Use NSGP

    1. Select the government service you need from the homepage or services page.
    2. Fill in the application form. Use the 🔊 speaker button beside each field to hear instructions.
    3. Use the 🎤 microphone button to fill fields using your voice.
    4. Click 📷 Scan Document to scan your documents using your camera. The system will automatically extract information.
    5. Review any validation errors and suggestions shown in real-time.
    6. The Pre-Submission Guardian will check your application before final submission.
    7. Once all checks pass, submit your application.

    Key Features

    • 🔍 Real-time validation as you type
    • 📷 Document scanning with OCR
    • 🔊 Audio instructions in multiple languages
    • 🎤 Voice input for form filling
    • 🛡️ Pre-Submission Guardian
    • 💾 Auto-save and resume
    • 📊 Form readiness score
